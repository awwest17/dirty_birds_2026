How the numbers are made: a single team's score swings about 28.0 points from week to week, while the real gap between teams looks more like 11.0 (assumed, until teams have two weeks each). Because the week-to-week noise is the larger of the two, one game of scoring is weak evidence — the league average is weighted like 6.5 games of a team's own results, and a team's projection only moves decisively once it has played enough for its scoring to outweigh that. The remaining schedule is then played 20,000 times, with playoff seeding broken by points for.

Early-season projections come from Sleeper's per-player numbers: each team's best available lineup sets its starting point, recentred on this league's own scoring so only the relative differences are borrowed. As real results accumulate they take over. Built September 15, 2026 at 7:48 PM. Team strength is estimated from scoring and, where available, published projections — it knows nothing about your league's trade chatter or who is about to be benched.
